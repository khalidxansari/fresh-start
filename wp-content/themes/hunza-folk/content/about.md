Hunza Folk is a digital archive of the folk music, instruments and musicians of Hunza Valley in Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan. There is currently no dedicated platform for Hunza's musical heritage, and this site is an attempt to build one.

## The valley

Hunza sits in the Karakoram mountains in the far north of Pakistan and is home to the Burusho people, who speak Burushaski. The region has its own music, instruments and oral traditions.

## The band

Hunza Folk is a folk music band formed in 2009 by young men from Hunza who were students at Aga Khan Higher Secondary School in Gilgit. They learned to play traditional Hunza instruments from local masters and began performing at school events, community gatherings and weddings. Members are now based in the UK, the United States and Germany.

## The archive

Everything here is free to read and listen to. Each song, artist and instrument has its own page, written for someone who has never heard of Hunza.
